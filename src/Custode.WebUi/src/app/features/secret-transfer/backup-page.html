Encrypted tenant backup

An encrypted copy of the whole tenant vault: folders, secrets with their versions, groups, grants, and policies. Account passwords and sessions are not included.

The backup cannot be opened without its passphrase, and the passphrase cannot be recovered.

Store the passphrase separately from the backup file. Custode does not keep it.
